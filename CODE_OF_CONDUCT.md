# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good faith, respect privacy, and focus criticism on ideas and code.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, sharing credentials or private customer information, and sustained disruption. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
